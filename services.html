SERVICE 1

Land Aggregation & Acquisition

Verified Industrial Land. Direct From Owners. Pan-India.

Land aggregation is the process of identifying, verifying, and consolidating land parcels — often from multiple owners — into one clean, contiguous, litigation-free block ready for industrial use. As professional land aggregators, that consolidation is our core expertise.

Your requirement, our search. Share your specifications — acreage, state/region preference, budget, industry type, infrastructure needs (highway access, power load, water table, labour availability) — and our ground network gets to work.

What we do

  • Requirement analysis & location advisory (which state's policies suit your industry best)
  • Ground-level land identification through our district-wise network
  • Direct owner/farmer contact — no chain of middlemen inflating rates
  • Title verification: ownership records, mutation, jamabandi/khatauni, encumbrance certificate
  • Litigation & dispute search
  • Soil testing, contour & feasibility assessment
  • Rate benchmarking against genuine circle rates and recent transactions
  • Negotiation & land tie-up (agreement to sell / MoU) on your behalf
  • Multi-owner land aggregation — consolidating adjacent parcels from several farmers/owners into one contiguous block
  • Registry & mutation support with legal documentation
What you get: A shortlist of verified, litigation-free, feasible aggregated land parcels at genuine rates — and a signed tie-up on the one you choose.

SERVICE 2

CLU & Land Conversion

From Agricultural to Industrial. Legally. Completely.

Buying land is 20% of the job. Making it legally usable for industry is the other 80% — and it's where most projects get stuck for years.

What we handle

  • Change of Land Use (CLU) application & processing with town planning / district authorities
  • Land use conversion under applicable state land revenue acts
  • Master plan & zonal plan compliance verification before you buy
  • Layout plan preparation & sanction
  • Coordination with Town & Country Planning, revenue department, and district administration
  • External development charges (EDC) & conversion fee calculation and advisory
Our promise: We tell you before purchase whether CLU is achievable on a parcel — so you never buy land you can't use.

Licenses, NOCs & Compliances

Every Approval Your Factory Needs. One Scope. Our Responsibility.

  • Factory License (under Factories Act) — application to grant
  • Pollution Board Clearances — Consent to Establish (CTE) & Consent to Operate (CTO)
  • Fire NOC — building plan compliance, hydrant systems coordination, inspection & clearance
  • Water — borewell/CGWA NOC, water connection, water audit compliance
  • Electricity — load sanction, connection, transformer & line coordination
  • Environment Clearance (where applicable) — EIA coordination
  • Building Plan Approvals — sanction from development authority
  • Labour & Shops registrations, MSME/Udyam registration
  • Completion & Occupancy Certificates
Milestone-tracked. Timeline-committed. You watch your file move on our client dashboard — no more "office ke chakkar."
