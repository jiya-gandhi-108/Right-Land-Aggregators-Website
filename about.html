Who We Are

Built to Replace the Broken Chain.

Right Land Aggregators and Developers Pvt. Ltd. is a professionally managed industrial land aggregation and development company headquartered in Delhi NCR, serving clients pan-India.

Why We Exist

India's Factories Are Expanding. The Land Process Isn't.

We were founded on a simple observation: India's factories are expanding faster than ever, but the land acquisition process hasn't changed in decades. It still runs on brokers, middlemen, verbal deals, hidden commissions, and endless government office visits. Expanding businesses lose crores — and worse, lose years — navigating a system with no accountability.

We built Right Land to replace that entire broken chain with one accountable land aggregator — a company that professionalises land aggregation for industrial India.
