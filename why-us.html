Why Right Land

Built to Replace the Broker System.

A broker sells you a plot. A land aggregator delivers you a verified, consolidated, approval-ready land solution. Here's what that difference means in practice:

1

One Disclosed Fee. In Writing. Before We Start.

Our search fee, success fee, and PMC rates are in the agreement you sign on day one. No cuts from the seller. No "setting" with dealers. If any vendor pays us a margin, it's disclosed in your contract.

2

You Pay the Landowner Directly.

Land payment goes from your account to the owner's account. We negotiate; we never touch the land money. The genuine rate is the rate you pay.

3

Responsibility Till the Key, Not Till the Registry.

A broker's job ends when he gets his commission. Our contractual scope ends when your facility is licensed, compliant, and handed over.

4

Fair to Farmers Too.

We pay landowners honest market rates. No exploitation, no pressure tactics. Clean deals close faster and never come back as disputes.

5

Pan-India Land Aggregation, One Standard.

Whether it's Haryana, Gujarat, UP, Tamil Nadu, or Assam — same aggregation process, same transparency, same dashboard.

6

Compliance-First, Always.

We are a registered private limited company. Every process we run is through official channels — applications, fees, receipts. We make files move with completeness and follow-up, never shortcuts.
