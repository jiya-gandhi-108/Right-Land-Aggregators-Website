How It Works

The Right Land Process — Requirement to Ribbon.

Seven steps, three phases, one dashboard. You always know exactly where your file is, what happens next, and when.

1

Tell Us Your Requirement

Fill the requirement form or meet our team. Industry, size, budget, preferred states, timeline.

2

Feasibility & Advisory Report

Within 10 days: which states/districts suit you best (land rates + subsidies + labour + logistics), realistic budget, realistic timeline.

3

Verified & Aggregated Options

Shortlisted parcels with title status, CLU feasibility, rate benchmarking, and site photos/videos. Adjacent plots aggregated into one contiguous block where needed. Site visits arranged.

4

Tie-Up & Due Diligence

You select; we negotiate directly with owners, complete legal verification, and sign the tie-up. You pay the owner directly — money never routes through us for the land itself.

5

Registry & Approvals

Registration, mutation, CLU, and the full license/NOC chain — tracked on your client dashboard.

6

Development (Optional)

Design, construction, interiors through empanelled vendors under our PMC.

7

Handover

Final compliance file (every license, every receipt, every drawing — indexed) + keys.

The Three Phases

Find → Approve → Build

The seven steps roll up into three committed phases — each with milestone-based payments and written timelines.

PHASE 1 · STEPS 1–3

Find

Requirement, feasibility report, and verified aggregated options — a shortlist worth visiting, not a pile of broker listings.

PHASE 2 · STEPS 4–5

Approve

Tie-up, due diligence, registry, mutation, CLU, and every license and NOC — the file moves, and you watch it move.

PHASE 3 · STEPS 6–7

Build

Optional development under our PMC, then handover: an indexed compliance file and the key in your hand.
