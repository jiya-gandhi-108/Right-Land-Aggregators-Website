Partnership Program

An Open Invitation to Every Industrial Association in India.

If you lead an industrial association, chamber of commerce, trade body, or manufacturers' forum — we invite you to tie up with Right Land. Your members get a single-window land partner. Your association earns a transparent, contractually agreed share of our professional fee on every engagement that comes through you.

How the Tie-Up Works

Sign Once. Earn on Every Member We Serve.

STEP 1

MoU & Onboarding

We sign a simple partnership MoU with your association — scope, referral process, and your fee share, all in writing. No investment, no obligation on your side.

STEP 2

Your Members Get Priority Service

Members access our full stack — land aggregation, CLU, licenses & NOCs, subsidies, and construction — with priority handling, dedicated relationship support, and preferential terms negotiated for your body.

STEP 3

Your Association Earns

On every member engagement routed through the association, we pay your body a pre-agreed proportion of our fee — disclosed in the MoU, settled milestone-wise, with full statements.
